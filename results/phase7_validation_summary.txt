8. HONEST ASSESSMENT OF 90% ACCURACY
   90% accuracy is NOT realistically achievable with this dataset without
   leakage or label manipulation. Reasons:
   - Strongest single-feature correlation is only r = 0.149 (cgpa).
   - Classes overlap heavily: placement depends on unobserved factors
     (interview performance, company demand, timing), not in the dataset.
   - ROC-AUC plateaus around 0.68-0.69 even after feature engineering and
     tuning; accuracy is bounded by the majority class (68.5%) plus the
     small learnable signal above it.
   - An always-predict-majority baseline already scores 68.5% accuracy;
     reported gains must be measured against THAT, not 90%.

9. BACKEND DECISION GUIDE
   - Use ROC-AUC (ranking quality) as the primary model-selection metric.
   - Use F1 as the tie-breaker; tune the decision threshold on business
     priorities (false-hope vs missed placement).
   - For a recommendation system, calibrated probabilities matter more
     than raw accuracy - prefer the model with the best ROC-AUC and
     use threshold + probability outputs in the app.
